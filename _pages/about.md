Julianna Sterling is an MSc student in Economics at Sciences Po, where she is an Eiffel Scholar. Her research and professional interests include banking and financial regulation, trade, energy economics, and applied microeconomics.

She previously worked as a Senior Research Assistant in the Federal Reserve Board’s Division of Supervision and Regulation, conducting empirical analysis on bank liquidity, long-term debt, and capital holdings in the context of Basel III Endgame. She has also researched microfinance and economic development in Dakar, Senegal and international immigration at the Cato Institute. Her internship experience includes a summer with the Auctions group of NERA Economic Consultancy (2026) and in international development contract bidding (2022). Her work has been published through the Federal Reserve Board’s FEDS Notes program.
